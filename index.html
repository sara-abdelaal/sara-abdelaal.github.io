ADAS · Autonomous Smart Vehicle
1ST PLACE - MTC IUGRC-9
Supervised by Dr. Karim Emara (Associate Professor of Computer Science @ ASU)
The ChallengeConsumer car hardware has no safety intelligence - no obstacle awareness, no driver-assist features, and no way to verify who's operating it.
The ApproachBuilt a dual-processor pipeline (Raspberry Pi + STM32) running YOLOv8 for object and sign detection, fused with LiDAR + camera sensor data for adaptive safety, plus a mobile telemetry app and a custom Face-ID system for biometric-secured ignition.

PolicyEye · AI Safety AND Monitoring System
ON-PREMISES
Advised by Dr. Mohamed Zorkany (Head of Electronics, NTI Egypt) & Dr. Ashraf Mohamed (AI Lead, Areeb Technology)
The ChallengeIndustrial sites need PPE-compliance monitoring, but cloud-based vision pipelines mean sending sensitive facility footage off-site - unacceptable for security-conscious operations.
